# English catalogue

## Punctuation and layout

- Em dash and en dash as punctuation (absolute). Use a comma, a period, parentheses, or rebuild the sentence.
- Colon setups in series: "The result:", "Here's the thing:", "The kicker:".
- Title Case On Every Heading when the house style is sentence case.
- Decorative bold, one bolded phrase per sentence.
- Emoji (absolute unless asked), including as bullet markers.
- Bullets where a paragraph reads better. "**Term**: explanation" on every line.

## Constructions

- "It's not X, it's Y" (absolute) and variants: "not just X, but Y", "more than X, it's Y", "X isn't about Y. It's about Z.", "less X, more Y".
- "No X. No Y. Just Z."
- Rhetorical question answered right away: "The result? A 20% increase." "The catch?"
- Reflexive groups of three.
- Staccato fragments for punch: "Simple. Fast. Reliable."
- One sentence per line with blank lines between (LinkedIn style).
- Openers: "In today's fast-paced world", "In an era of", "Imagine a world where", "Have you ever wondered", "Whether you're X or Y".
- Signposting: "In this article, we'll explore", "Let's dive in", "Here's why", "Let's break it down".
- "Here's where X comes in", "This is where it gets interesting", "But that's not all".
- Summary closers: "In conclusion", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day", "In summary".
- Inspirational closers and engagement bait: "The future belongs to those who", "What do you think? Let me know in the comments."

## Hedges and filler

- "It's worth noting", "It's important to note", "It's important to remember", "Notably", "Interestingly".
- "may potentially", "could possibly", "in some cases", "to some extent", "arguably".
- "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally" in series.
- Intensifiers: "truly", "really", "deeply", "incredibly", "absolutely", "genuinely", "honestly".

## Banned vocabulary

delve, dive into, tapestry, landscape (figurative), realm, ecosystem (figurative), journey (figurative), navigate (figurative), leverage, harness, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, supercharge, streamline, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changer, revolutionize, transformative, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, holistic, synergy, testament to, plays a crucial role, foster, bustling, vibrant, meticulous, intricate, embark, resonate, showcase, in the realm of, a myriad of, ever-evolving.

## Leftovers (absolute)

"Certainly!", "Great question", "I hope this helps", "As an AI", "Here's a revised version", placeholders in brackets, Markdown symbols in a plain-text channel.

## Rhythm and tone

- Uniform sentence and paragraph length.
- Nominalizations ("the implementation of the optimization of") instead of verbs.
- Passive voice when the actor is known.
- Forced enthusiasm, flattering the reader, superlatives without evidence.
