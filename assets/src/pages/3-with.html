How a project goes

  1. Watch

    A few days where the service happens today, with the paper and the queue.

  2. Redraw

    The fewest questions that still get the job done, sketched on paper first.

  3. Test with five people

    Five sessions find most of the problems. Then I fix and test again.

  4. Hand over

    Specs your developers can build from, and I stay reachable during the build.
