The mentions French law asks for, filled in.

Forget one and the invoice can be refused. Carnet writes them from your profile and your client's.

SIRET
Yours and your client's, checked against the official register.
VAT
Your VAT number, or "TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI" if you are exempt.
Late payment
The penalty rate, and the €40 fixed recovery fee owed by business clients.
Numbering
One unbroken sequence per year. A deleted invoice becomes a credit note.
